Read Whale Flow Without Getting Faked Out

A simple, repeatable way to read options flow before you risk money. Flow is a radar ping — not a trade signal.

1) The 30-Second Flow Decoder

A. What happened?
Sweep = urgent fills across exchanges (often momentum/immediacy).
Block = negotiated large trade (often institution-sized).
Premium ($) = size/importance — confirm context (open interest, liquidity).
B. Why did it happen?
Speculation (directional bet) · Hedge (protection against a move) · Roll (close one position, open another).
Same print, three meanings. Guess wrong and you fade a hedge.
C. Where does price matter?
You only act around levels:
support / resistance · VWAP · prior-day high/low · 20/50/200 MA.

2) 5 Rules That Stop You From Getting Cooked

  1. Flow + structure + confirmation. Need all three. One is noise.
  2. Trade the level, not the story. Entries live at breaks and holds, not mid-range.
  3. Define invalidation first. Know where you are proven wrong before you enter.
  4. Size smaller when IV is high. Premium lies when volatility is rich.
  5. No revenge trades. One clean setup beats ten forced ones.

4) The 3-Line Plan (Write It Before You Click)

Line 1 — Trigger: the break/hold level that activates your idea
Line 2 — Target: the next level
Line 3 — Invalidation: where you exit if you are wrong
Example — Trigger: reclaim 610 · Target: 615 · Invalidation: lose 600

5) Two Questions to Ask Any Scan

"Is the index helping or fighting me?"
Index bias is the first filter. A perfect ticker-level setup against the tape is a trap, not a trade.
"Where is my stop, in dollars?"
If you cannot size the position from the invalidation line, you are not ready to enter.
